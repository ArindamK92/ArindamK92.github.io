About

Research-led, student-centered, and focused on systems that matter.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the CReWMaN Lab at Missouri University of Science and Technology, where I work with Prof. Sajal K. Das on scalable graph algorithms, dynamic networks, and cyber-physical applications.

My research interests span high-performance data analytics, parallel and distributed computing, graph analytics, scalable artificial intelligence, heterogeneous computing, and cyber-physical and data-intensive systems.

I also enjoy teaching and mentoring students. During my PhD, I taught and supported courses in algorithms, databases, cloud computing, big data, and advances in cyber-physical systems.

Research

Core themes

Graph analytics at scale

Parallel and incremental algorithms for shortest paths, coloring, strongly connected components, PageRank, and hypergraph analytics in large dynamic networks.

Scalable AI

Efficient learning pipelines for evolving and data-intensive systems, including semi-supervised learning and graph-informed AI methods.

Cyber-physical systems

Optimization and decision-making for EV charging, Urban Air Mobility, wildfire logistics, and other CPS applications where algorithmic efficiency matters.

Heterogeneous computing

CUDA, OpenMP, Open MPI, and SYCL implementations designed to exploit modern CPU-GPU and distributed HPC architectures.

Mentoring

What I look for in PhD students

I value students who are curious, persistent, and willing to work on problems that combine theory, systems, and real data. A strong fit usually includes one or more of the following:

  • Interest in HPC, distributed systems, or algorithm design
  • Comfort with Python, C++, Java, or performance-oriented programming
  • Motivation to work on graph analytics, AI, or CPS applications
  • Willingness to read papers, prototype ideas, and iterate rigorously

If you are interested, email me with your CV, transcript, and a short note about the problems you want to work on.
